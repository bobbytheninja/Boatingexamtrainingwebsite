# Favicon Concepts for Black Sea Bulgaria

## Current Design (in use)
**Classic Yacht** - Traditional sailboat with dual sails, hull, mast, and wave accents
- File: `/src/app/public/favicon.svg`
- Style: Detailed, recognizable, classic maritime

## Alternative Concepts

### Concept 1: Minimal
**File:** `concept-1-minimal.svg`
- Single geometric sail
- Ultra-clean design
- Best for: Modern, tech-forward brand
- Pros: Scales well at tiny sizes, very recognizable
- Cons: Less detail, might feel too simple

### Concept 2: Anchor
**File:** `concept-2-anchor.svg`
- Traditional nautical anchor symbol
- Professional and established
- Best for: Authority, maritime certification focus
- Pros: Universal maritime symbol, strong visual
- Cons: Less dynamic than yacht/boat imagery

### Concept 3: Compass
**File:** `concept-3-compass.svg`
- Navigation compass rose with red north pointer
- Emphasizes navigation/direction
- Best for: Navigation exam focus, exploration theme
- Pros: Circular design fits well in browser tabs, navigation theme
- Cons: More complex, might lose detail at small sizes

### Concept 4: Waves
**File:** `concept-4-waves.svg`
- Layered abstract waves with small yacht
- Emphasizes water/maritime environment
- Best for: Coastal, relaxed vibe
- Pros: Unique visual, creates depth
- Cons: Might be too abstract for instant recognition

### Concept 5: Ship's Helm (Wheel)
**File:** `concept-5-helm.svg`
- Classic ship's steering wheel
- Professional maritime symbol
- Best for: Control, expertise, professional training
- Pros: Instantly recognizable maritime symbol, symmetrical
- Cons: Complex details at small sizes

## Usage

To test any concept:
1. Copy the concept file to `/src/app/public/favicon.svg`
2. Update other icon sizes (192, 512, apple-touch) with scaled versions
3. Deploy and test

## Recommendations

- **For maximum recognition:** Stick with current yacht or use Concept 2 (Anchor)
- **For modern/minimal brand:** Use Concept 1 (Minimal)
- **For navigation emphasis:** Use Concept 3 (Compass)
- **For professional authority:** Use Concept 5 (Helm)
