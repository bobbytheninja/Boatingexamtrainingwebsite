## 🎨 File Upload Requirements

### File Format:
- **PDF** (best - preserves quality)
- **AI** (Adobe Illustrator)
- **EPS** (vector format)
- **SVG** (can work, but convert to PDF to be safe)

### How to Prepare Files:

1. **Insert your QR code** into the SVG designs
   - Replace "qr-code.png" reference with your actual QR
   - Use Inkscape (free) or Illustrator

2. **Convert to PDF:**
   - Open SVG in Inkscape
   - File → Save As → PDF
   - Select "Convert texts to paths" option
   - Save with high resolution (300 DPI minimum)

3. **Upload to Print24:**
   - They will check files
   - May contact you if any issues
   - Usually approve within 1 business day
